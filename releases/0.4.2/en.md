Improved performance

- Faster repeated history searches and pagination, with cancellation of obsolete searches.
- Smoother scrolling through large diffs: only visible rows are rendered.
- Faster folder selection and staging in repositories with many changed files.
- Folder diffs preserve the current file's position while neighboring files load.
- Cached diffs open faster when switching between files; worktree changes invalidate the cache.
- Conflict result highlighting stays lightweight even in large files.
